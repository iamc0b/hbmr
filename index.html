    Runner card

    Data. Results, splits and pairings from the club's HBMR results sheet. Field positions on splits are shown where the sheet records them (2009–2010, 2015–2024). 2001 cancelled (foot and mouth), 2008 no record, 2019 entry missed, 2020 COVID, 2025 cancelled for storm. In 2013 the footbridge control has no time, so its split is merged into Hart Crag.

    Route. Checkpoints from the six-figure grid references on hbmr.org.uk/leg-routes. Lines between checkpoints follow the usual paths but are hand-drawn, not GPS tracks.

    Terrain. For true elevations, download OS Terrain 50 (free OpenData, format "ASCII Grid and GML (Grid)", coverage all of GB) from the OS Data Hub. Load the whole downloaded zip with the button under the map, or for speed just the four tile zips from its data/ny folder (ny30, ny31, ny40, ny41). The page picks out the Patterdale tiles itself.
